How to walk

A classic minefield, dressed as a night garden. Open every safe bed. Pins mark seeds.

Reveal
Left click or tap. First step is always safe, and prefers a clearing.
Pin
Right click, long-press, or F. On touch, switch the HUD to Flag.
Chord
Middle click, left+right, Q, Shift+Space, or click a number whose pins match.
Move
Arrows or WASD. Space / Enter opens. Esc closes. H heat pulse.
Heat
Ripple of adjacent-seed density. Does not open tiles. Limited uses.
Daily
One seeded Grove per UTC day. Same first cell, same garden.
Zen
No clock. Migration (off by default) lets unpinned seeds sidestep after a reveal, never changing numbers you already see.
Ghost
After a loss, walk the roots — seeds show as hairline roots. Not a second life.

Plant a garden

Choose a bed. Daily uses Grove, seeded from the UTC date.
