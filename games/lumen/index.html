How to play

Touch

  • Drag sideways anywhere: the piece follows your finger, one cell at a time.
  • Tap: rotate. Left half turns it left, right half turns it right (or any tap turns right; see Settings).
  • Two-finger tap: rotate left.
  • Flick down: hard drop. Drag down slowly: soft drop.
  • Flick up: hold.
  • Prefer buttons? Settings → Controls → Buttons.

Keyboard

  • ← → move   ↓ soft drop   Space hard drop
  • ↑ / X rotate right   Z rotate left   A rotate 180
  • C / Shift hold   Esc pause   R restart

Scoring

  • Clear four lines at once for a Tetris. Twist a T into a slot for a T-spin.
  • Tetrises and T-spins in a row are back-to-back (×1.5). Clears on consecutive pieces build a combo.
  • Empty the whole well for a Perfect Clear.

Worlds

Every few levels the world changes: Dawn Tide, Aurora Veil, Neon Rain, Crystal Hollow, Deep Field and the Solar Crown. The music grows with you.

On iPhone and iPad

Open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. LUMEN then runs full screen and works offline. If the music is silent, check the ring/silent switch.
