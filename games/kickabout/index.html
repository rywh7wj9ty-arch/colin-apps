Controls

Touch: drag anywhere on the left side to run. Tap the KICK button to pass. Hold it to charge a shot, longer hold = harder and higher. Swipe sideways as you let go, or steer straight after kicking, to bend the ball. Tap KICK without the ball to slide tackle (close) or switch player (far).

2 players: each player gets half the screen (bottom/top in portrait, left/right in landscape). Run with the outer side of your half, kick with the inner side.

Keyboard: P1 WASD + Space kick, Left Shift switch. P2 arrows + Enter kick, Right Shift switch. Esc pauses.
