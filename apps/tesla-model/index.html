How the model works
Each business line follows an S-curve from its 2026 level to a long-run cap (midpoint year + steepness). Revenue = volume × price (cars, FSD subscriptions, robotaxi miles × fare, Optimus units, storage GWh × $/kWh, services). EBITDA = segment margins (ramping to mature over 6 years) minus unallocated R&D. Equity value in year Y = next year's EBITDA × an EV/EBITDA multiple that shrinks from the 2027 level to a mature level by 2038, plus net cash; the price converges from today's market price over 3 years. SpaceX is modelled the same way from revenue. After the merger, each TSLA share becomes ratio SPCX shares of the combined company (combined value × (1+synergy) ÷ combined shares).
