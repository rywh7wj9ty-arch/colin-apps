Visit tips

  • Pre-book online only — day tickets are limited. Check elmleynaturereserve.co.uk before travelling.
  • Address: Kingshill Farm, Isle of Sheppey, Kent ME12 3RN / ME12 3RW · What3Words mystified.topping.pricing · ~40 miles from London.
  • Entry track (~2 miles): stay in your car and use it as a hide — do not get out on the drive.
  • Walk only marked trails. No dogs except registered assistance dogs.
  • Typical hours (always check website): Oct–late May 9–5; late May–end Sep 9–4; summer Sundays 11–6.
  • Four hides (no booking): Well Marsh, Counterwall, South Fleet, Spitend (~5.5 km from farm — take a picnic).
  • Facilities: visitor car park, Kings Hill Farm, Linhay (tea/cake; binocular hire ~£5/day), picnic benches past the car park. Barn/accommodation for overnight guests only.
  • Wildlife: Lapwing, Pied Avocet, Redshank, Marsh Harrier, Barn & Little Owl, Brown Hare, Egrets; winter wildfowl; spring/autumn waders.
  • History: Philip & Corinne Merricks restored nature over 40+ years; Gareth & Georgina Fulton continue — only family-owned NNR in the UK (~1,212 ha / 3,300 acres).
